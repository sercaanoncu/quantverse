# QuantVerse Thesis Style Source Audit

Retrieval date: 2026-07-02

This audit records external academic-writing sources used to translate
doctoral-dissertation and defense-presentation conventions into the QuantVerse
doctoral-style research package. The sources are used as formatting and
structure guidance only. They are not used as performance evidence, financial
data, market data or investment advice.

## QuantVerse Formatting Translation

The dissertation output follows a hybrid structure:

1. Doctoral monograph front matter: title page, Turkish abstract, English
   abstract, extended executive summary, table-of-contents-style overview and
   glossary.
2. Scientific report core: research problem, research questions, methodology,
   data governance, risk diagnostics, results and discussion.
3. Technical appendices: commands, schemas, FX formula, market-cap/rank
   evidence rules, model status, requirement traceability and non-advice
   disclaimer.

The defense presentation follows a 27-slide academic defense arc: motivation,
problem, questions, contribution, architecture, methods, results, why not
promoted, limitations, red-team review, future work and conclusion.

## Academic Guardrails

- The thesis may be doctoral-style, but it must not pretend to be an accepted
  university dissertation.
- Formatting guidance is not financial validation.
- Web sources inform structure only; QuantVerse performance, blockers and
  decisions come from repo files and generated evidence outputs.
- No long copyrighted text from web pages or books is copied.
