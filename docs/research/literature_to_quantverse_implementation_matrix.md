# Literature-To-QuantVerse Implementation Matrix

This matrix maps the research conclusions used by the project into current
implementation status. It intentionally avoids fabricated citation claims. It
records what is implemented, what is missing, and whether the missing item
should be implemented now.

| Research area | Literature insight | QuantVerse current implementation | Gap | Recommended action | Priority | Implementation complexity | Expected impact | Risk of overfitting | Should implement now? | If no, why not? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Equal Weight / 1/N | 1/N is hard to beat when expected returns are noisy. | Equal Weight is a benchmark and broad default champion candidate. | None material. | Keep as mandatory benchmark. | High | Low | High | Low | yes | N/A |
| Mean-Variance Optimization | Return-estimation error can dominate optimization. | Mean-variance and Max Sharpe exist with shrinkage diagnostics. | Expected returns remain fragile. | Keep diagnostic unless promotion gates pass. | High | Medium | Medium | High | yes | N/A |
| Black-Litterman | Priors can stabilize views, but views must be defensible. | Not promoted in production path. | No external market-cap prior or validated view process. | Document as future work. | Medium | Medium | Medium | Medium | no | Current data lacks a defensible external prior. |
| HRP / HERC / NCO | Hierarchical methods avoid covariance inversion and can improve stability. | HRP and Signal-Aware HRP Lite are risk candidates. | HERC/NCO not implemented. | Keep HRP; consider HERC/NCO later. | Medium | Medium | Medium | Medium | no | Additional variants would expand multiple-testing risk. |
| Risk Parity | Risk allocation can reduce concentration in return forecasts. | Risk Parity portfolio exists. | Needs same league/promotion interpretation as defensive candidate. | Keep as defensive/risk allocation engine. | High | Low | Medium | Low | yes | N/A |
| CVaR / Expected Shortfall optimization | Tail-risk allocation can be more relevant than variance. | CVaR optimizer and VaR/CVaR risk reporting exist. | ES backtesting not yet implemented. | Add ES backtesting later. | Medium | Medium | Medium | Medium | no | Requires careful exception design and enough tail observations. |
| Shrinkage covariance | Shrinkage improves stability versus sample covariance. | Ledoit-Wolf is the promoted covariance input. | None material. | Keep and document. | High | Low | High | Low | yes | N/A |
| Nonlinear shrinkage | Can improve covariance eigenvalue stability. | Not implemented. | Requires extra estimator and validation. | Future upgrade. | Medium | Medium | Medium | Low | no | Not necessary before validating current risk engines. |
| Factor covariance | Institutional risk teams often prefer factor structure. | Factor risk decomposition exists; factor covariance is roadmap. | No factor covariance allocator. | Future institutional extension. | Medium | High | High | Medium | no | Needs robust factor data and governance. |
| EWMA / DCC-GARCH / dynamic covariance | Recent volatility can matter more than old volatility. | EWMA estimator exists and comparison table is produced. | EWMA not promoted as default. | Keep comparison; validate before promotion. | Medium | Low | Medium | Medium | yes | N/A |
| Time-Series Momentum | Trend persistence can be a defensible active-return family. | Time-Series Momentum challenger is implemented walk-forward. | Needs continuing out-of-sample evidence. | Keep in alpha league. | High | Low | High | Medium | yes | N/A |
| Cross-Asset Momentum | Relative strength can rank assets without forecasting levels. | Cross-Asset Relative Momentum is implemented walk-forward. | Needs subperiod and cost robustness. | Keep in alpha league. | High | Low | High | Medium | yes | N/A |
| Asset-Class Momentum Rotation | Asset-class rotation is simpler and more stable than single-asset timing. | Asset-Class Momentum Rotation is implemented and separately labeled. | Needs future unseen data confirmation. | Keep as annual-return challenger when gates pass. | High | Low | High | Medium | yes | N/A |
| Trend-Following | Moving-average rules can reduce exposure in weak trends. | Trend-Following MA challenger is implemented. | Uses simple price proxy from returns. | Keep as transparent challenger. | Medium | Low | Medium | Medium | yes | N/A |
| Volatility Targeting | Risk scaling can improve drawdown control but may reduce CAGR. | Volatility-scaled momentum and risk-managed EW exist. | No portfolio-level leverage target. | Keep unlevered; consider explicit target later. | Medium | Medium | Medium | Medium | yes | N/A |
| Regime Detection | Regime labels can help risk control but can overfit. | Regime-aware rules and regime diagnostics exist. | No promoted regime model champion. | Keep diagnostic until robust. | Medium | Medium | Medium | High | yes | N/A |
| Stress Testing | Scenario analysis identifies nonlinear portfolio risk. | Stress scenario table and report sections exist. | Needs more macro scenarios for institutions. | Extend later. | High | Low | High | Low | yes | N/A |
| VaR Backtesting | Exception tests validate risk forecasts. | VaR exception testing exists. | Limited by sample length and model assumptions. | Keep and explain. | High | Low | High | Low | yes | N/A |
| ES Backtesting | ES is harder to backtest but more tail-aware. | Not implemented. | Requires additional methodology. | Future risk upgrade. | Medium | Medium | Medium | Low | no | Current sprint focuses on architecture and alpha league. |
| ML Downside Risk | ML can be useful as a diagnostic or overlay. | Downside-risk ML layer exists. | Not tied to allocation veto yet. | Keep diagnostic; design overlay later. | High | Medium | Medium | Medium | yes | N/A |
| Meta-labeling / trade filter | Filtering decisions may be more realistic than predicting returns. | Documented as next ML step. | Not implemented. | Future supervised overlay. | Medium | Medium | Medium | Medium | no | Requires labels and purged validation design. |
| Regularized ML: Ridge/LASSO/Elastic Net | Regularization should precede deep learning on small noisy data. | Not implemented as allocation signal. | No feature/label governance yet. | Future ML extension. | Medium | Medium | Medium | Medium | no | Avoids adding weak models without validation. |
| Tree/boosting models | Nonlinear models can fit regimes but overfit easily. | Current ML is diagnostic. | No cost-aware allocation overlay. | Future diagnostic comparison only. | Low | Medium | Medium | High | no | Risk of feature/data snooping. |
| LSTM / Transformer time-series forecasting | Deep sequence models need large data and strict validation. | Explicitly not implemented as allocation engines. | None; exclusion is deliberate. | Keep as future research only. | Low | High | Low | High | no | Current data and validation do not justify them. |
| Reinforcement learning | RL allocation is research-stage and execution-sensitive. | Not implemented. | No simulator, execution model or policy governance. | Do not add now. | Low | High | Low | High | no | Would overcomplicate and overstate the project. |
| LLM-based finance research | LLMs can support research and text features, not autonomous allocation. | Documented governance role only. | No text data pipeline. | Future RAG/sentiment notebook. | Low | Medium | Medium | High | no | No validated text-to-portfolio pipeline exists. |
| Transaction-cost-aware backtesting | Net performance matters more than gross performance. | Costs, turnover and 25/50 bps challenger checks exist. | Execution/slippage/tax not modeled institutionally. | Keep and expand later. | High | Medium | High | Medium | yes | N/A |
| Purged CV / CPCV | Avoids leakage for overlapping labels and many trials. | Documented validation roadmap. | Not implemented. | Add when ML labels require it. | Medium | Medium | High | Low | no | Current walk-forward alpha rules do not use overlapping supervised labels. |
| Deflated Sharpe / PBO | Multiple testing can turn noise into false winners. | Lightweight overfit diagnostics exist. | Full DSR/PBO not implemented. | Future statistical validation upgrade. | Medium | Medium | High | Low | no | Needs a defined strategy-trial set and more statistical machinery. |
| Model governance / MLOps | Model approval requires auditability and limits. | Model cards, limitations, reproducibility and transfer manifests exist. | No live monitoring, approval workflow or access control. | Keep governance docs honest. | High | Medium | High | Low | yes | N/A |
