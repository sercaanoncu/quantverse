# Do Not Do

- Do not fabricate tickers.
- Do not fabricate market caps or ranks.
- Do not fabricate source URLs.
- Do not claim guaranteed returns or guaranteed outperformance.
- Do not provide investment advice.
- Do not commit generated outputs from `data/processed`.
- Do not broadly refactor without a concrete need.
- Do not add learning-path, interview, CV-score or personal branding docs.
- Do not add mandatory XGBoost, LightGBM or TensorFlow dependencies.
- Do not treat current constituents as point-in-time historical evidence.
